Where's the Key?

Real-time musical key detection for Android.

About the app

Where's the Key? listens through your microphone and tells you, in real time, which musical key you're playing in. Point it at a piano, guitar, or any instrument, play a few notes, and get an instant answer — no setup, no account.

  • Instant, on-device key detection
  • Works with any acoustic or digital instrument
  • Audio is processed live and never stored or uploaded
