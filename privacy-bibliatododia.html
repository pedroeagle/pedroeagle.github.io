Privacy Policy — Bíblia Todo Dia

Also known as “Bible Every Day” · Last updated: June 2025

Bíblia Todo Dia / Bible Every Day (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the “App”).

Information We Collect

1. Automatically Collected Information

Device Information:

  • Device type and operating system (iOS/Android)
  • Device language and locale settings
  • App version and usage analytics

Push Notification Data:

  • Expo push notification token (for sending reading reminders)
  • Notification preferences and settings

2. User-Generated Information

Reading Progress Data:

  • Your selected Bible reading plan
  • Reading completion status for each day
  • Start date of your reading plan
  • Reading statistics and progress

App Settings:

  • Preferred Bible version and language
  • Theme preferences (dark/light mode)
  • App language settings

3. Technical Data

Local Storage:

  • All reading progress is stored locally on your device using MMKV storage
  • Bible plans and content cache
  • User preferences and settings

How We Use Your Information

  • Core functionality: provide personalized Bible reading plans, track and display your reading progress, and sync your preferences across sessions
  • Notifications: send reading reminders and motivational messages, and deliver plan-specific notifications based on your selections
  • App improvement: analyze usage patterns and optimize performance and user experience
  • Content delivery: download and cache Bible content for offline use and provide relevant Bible versions in your preferred language

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

  • All personal data is stored locally on your device
  • Reading progress, preferences, and settings never leave your device
  • Local data is protected using device-level security

Server Communication

We only communicate with our servers to:

  • Download Bible reading plans
  • Verify content updates
  • Register for push notifications
  • Check for app updates

Data Retention

  • Local data persists until you uninstall the app or reset your plan
  • Server-side data (push tokens) is automatically removed when tokens expire
  • No personal reading data is retained on our servers

Third-Party Services

YouVersion Bible App — Purpose: open specific Bible passages. Data shared: Bible version and passage references only. Policy: bible.com/privacy

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — Purpose: display relevant advertisements. Data: advertising identifiers for personalized ads. Policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can opt out of ad tracking through your device's advertising settings.

Bible.com API — Purpose: fetch available Bible versions. Data shared: language preference only.

Expo Notifications — Purpose: push notification delivery. Data shared: device push token and plan selections. Policy: expo.dev/privacy

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences and contains religious content suitable for all ages. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

Your Privacy Rights

  • Access: view all data stored locally through the app's settings
  • Delete: remove all local data by resetting your plan or uninstalling the app
  • Modify: change your preferences and settings at any time
  • Opt-out: disable push notifications through app or device settings

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share anonymized, aggregated data for app analytics and improvement, and to understand user preferences for content development.

International Data Transfers

Our app operates globally. If you're located outside the country where our servers are hosted, your information may be transferred internationally. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for any international transfers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the new policy in the app and updating the “Last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at pedroeagledev@gmail.com.

Advertising and Analytics

Personalized Ads: we use Google Mobile Ads to display relevant advertisements. Ad personalization can be controlled through your device settings. We do not share reading content or progress with advertising partners.

Analytics: we collect anonymized usage data to improve app performance. No personal reading data is included in analytics, and all analytics data is aggregated and cannot identify individual users.

By using Bíblia Todo Dia / Bible Every Day, you consent to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.
