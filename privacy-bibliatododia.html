Privacy Policy — Bíblia Todo Dia

Last updated: August 2026

Bíblia Todo Dia (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Bíblia Todo Dia (the “App”).

Information We Collect

1. Automatically Collected Information

Device Information:

  • Device type and operating system (iOS/Android)
  • Device language and locale settings
  • App version

Advertising Data:

  • The App displays ads through Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and general device/usage data to serve and measure ads (see “Advertising” below)

2. Notifications

  • With your permission, the App sends daily reading reminders and devotional notifications
  • Push notifications are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google). To deliver them, a device push token may be generated and processed
  • You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings

3. User-Generated Information

App Settings & Content:

  • Reading progress, bookmarks, and favorite verses
  • Notification/reminder preferences and schedule
  • Theme preferences (dark/light mode) and app language

4. Technical Data

Local Storage: Reading progress, preferences, and settings are stored locally on your device using on-device storage.

Audio Playback

The App can play audio (such as narrated Bible content). This uses your device's speaker/audio output only — the App does not use your microphone and does not record any audio.

Advertising

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and general device and usage information, in accordance with Google's policies. Depending on your consent and region, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • You can reset or opt out of your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads (Android).

How We Use Your Information

  • Core functionality: display Bible reading content, track your reading progress, play audio, and sync your preferences across sessions
  • Notifications: send the daily reminders and devotionals you opt in to
  • Advertising: display ads via Google AdMob to support the App
  • App improvement: optimize performance and user experience

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob — Purpose: display advertising. Data: advertising identifier, IP address, device/usage data. Policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — Purpose: deliver push notifications. Data: device push token. Policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Expo — Purpose: app framework/runtime and notifications. Data: standard device information. Policy: expo.dev/privacy

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. Data is shared only with Google AdMob (advertising) and Google Firebase (push notification delivery), as described above. Your reading data and preferences are stored on your device.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

Your Privacy Rights

  • Access / Modify: view and change your settings within the app
  • Delete: remove all local data by clearing app data or uninstalling
  • Control: enable or disable notifications, and control ad personalization, through your device settings

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the new policy and updating the “Last updated” date.

Contact

By using Bíblia Todo Dia, you consent to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.
