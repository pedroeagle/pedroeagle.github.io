Privacy Policy

Where's the Key? · Last updated: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Where's the Key? ("the App"), developed by Pedro Igor ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Microphone & audio

The App uses your device's microphone to detect musical notes and identify the key you are playing in. Audio is analyzed in real time on your device only. We do not record, save, store, or transmit any audio to our servers or to any third party. Nothing you play is retained after it is processed.

2. Information we collect

We do not require an account and we do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos, or location data. The App itself does not gather personally identifiable information.

3. Advertising

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and process certain data, such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and general device and usage information, in accordance with Google's own policies. This may be used to provide personalized or non-personalized ads.

You can learn more, and control ad personalization, here:

  • How Google uses information from partner apps
  • Your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads (reset or opt out of your advertising ID)

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your data. The only third-party service that receives data is Google AdMob, as described above, strictly to display advertising within the App.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at pedroigor.silva@outlook.com.
